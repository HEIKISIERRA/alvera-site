# ALVERA — Prompts de imágenes

Cada imagen corresponde a un `.ph[data-scene]` de `index.html`. Para usarla, añade `data-img="ruta/foto.jpg"` a ese elemento.

## Sufijo de estilo (pegar al final de TODOS los prompts)
```
editorial interior design magazine photography, natural side light from tall windows, soft long shadows,
warm restrained palette (bone, sand, greige, light oak, charcoal, touches of aged brass and terracotta),
noble materials (travertine, oak, linen, lime plaster, aged brass), sculptural minimal furniture,
shot on 35mm / medium format, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, calm, timeless, no people, no text, no logos
--no stock photo look, no real-estate wide-angle distortion, no clutter, no saturated colors
```

## Hero (16:9, 3200×1800) — `hero`
Sunlit residential living room, late afternoon. Tall floor-to-ceiling windows on the right casting long diagonal light across an oak floor. Curved bouclé sofa in greige, round travertine side table, an arched plaster niche on the left with a ceramic vase and a dry branch, one abstract artwork with a terracotta circle on the wall. Low camera, composition leaves empty space on the left for large headline text.

## Statement — detalle (4:5) — `plaster`
Close-up of a lime-washed plaster wall with a soft curved shadow and warm light grazing from the upper right. Abstract, tactile, almost no objects.

## Proyecto destacado — Residencia Olivo (3:2) — `living`
Same house as the hero, wider and brighter: living area with window light, sofa, travertine table, art on wall, plant at the right edge.

## Detalle de latón (1:1) — `brass`
Vertical aged-brass door pull on a pale warm plaster wall, raking light from the right, patina and fingerprints visible.

## Portafolio
- **Residencia Olivo (4:5)** — vertical view of the living room, window at right, sofa and art, floor reflections.
- **Casa Sal (3:2)** — beach house: three tall arched openings looking to a pale sea and sky, sand-coloured plaster walls, low travertine bench, terracotta pot with a branch.
- **Hotel Bruma (7:10)** — boutique hotel bedroom at dusk: bouclé headboard, linen bedding, brass bedside lamp glowing, dark walnut and charcoal walls, a slit of warm light from the curtain.
- **Estudio Calma (12:5, panoramic)** — boutique office: continuous window band with black steel mullions, long oak table, two simple chairs, a bookshelf with muted book spines.
- **Restaurante Ámbar (1:1)** — restaurant at night: three brass pendant lamps with amber glow, long oak table with plates, terracotta arched wall, dark floor.

## Método (4:5) — 5 imágenes
1. **Listen** `ceramic` — still life: pale ceramic vase with a dry branch and a terracotta dish on a stone shelf, soft window light.
2. **Concept** `stone` — macro of honed travertine slab, horizontal veining, warm raking light.
3. **Design** `plaster` — same as statement detail, different crop.
4. **Craft** `wood` — brushed European oak planks, close-up, side light showing the grain.
5. **Reveal** `living2` — finished living room, cooler greige palette, window at left, sofa, one art piece, no plant.

## Materialidad

- **Muestras:** travertina (4:5), roble (1:1), lino belga sin teñir (3:2, soft folds), latón envejecido (1:1).
