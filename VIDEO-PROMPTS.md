# ALVERA — Prompts de video

La web actual no usa video: son clips opcionales. Todos son loops silenciosos, lentos y casi imperceptibles (la web respira, no se mueve). Genera cada clip con la foto correspondiente de IMAGE-PROMPTS.md como primer fotograma (image-to-video) para mantener coherencia.

## Sufijo común (al final de cada prompt)
```
cinematic interior film, 35mm, slow and calm, locked-off tripod shot or an extremely slow dolly,
natural window light, soft long shadows, warm restrained palette (bone, sand, greige, oak, charcoal, aged brass),
subtle film grain, seamless loop, no people, no text, no logos, no fast motion, no camera shake
```
**Especificaciones:** 5–8 s, 24 fps, 1080p mínimo (4K para el hero), MP4 H.264 + WebM, sin audio, <3 MB por clip en mobile (versión 720p).

---

## 1. Hero (16:9) — sustituye la foto del hero
Sunlit living room in late afternoon. Light from tall right-side windows slowly shifts across the oak floor and travertine table; dust motes drift in the beam; the dry branch in the ceramic vase sways almost imperceptibly. Camera: ultra-slow push-in (2% over 8 s). Leave the left third calm and uncluttered for headline text.

## 2. Luz en el suelo (9:16 y 16:9) — fondo del manifiesto
Close-up of a lime-washed plaster wall and oak floor. A band of sunlight slowly travels across the surface as clouds pass; shadows of a window mullion glide and soften. Static camera.

## 3. Residencia Olivo (3:2) — proyecto destacado
Slow lateral dolly (left to right, 10 cm) through the living room: bouclé sofa, travertine side table, arched niche with vase. Light changes subtly from warm gold to soft amber.

## 4. Casa Sal (3:2) — portafolio
Three arched openings framing a pale sea. Gentle sea breeze moves a linen curtain at the edge; reflected water light ripples faintly on the plaster ceiling. Static camera.

## 5. Hotel Bruma (7:10) — portafolio
Boutique hotel bedroom at dusk. The brass bedside lamp glows with a very slight warm flicker; a slit of curtain light drifts across the linen bedding. Camera: slow push toward the headboard.

## 6. Estudio Calma (12:5) — portafolio panorámico
Panoramic office window band; soft clouds pass outside and light slides slowly across the long oak table. Lateral dolly at walking speed, very slow.

## 7. Restaurante Ámbar (1:1) — portafolio
Amber pendant lamps over a long oak table. Slight breathing glow in the lamps, candle flames steady, faint steam from a cup. Slow push-in.

## 8. Método — 5 microclips (4:5, 4 s)
1. **Listen:** ceramic vase on a stone shelf, window light shifts, branch barely sways.
2. **Concept:** macro of travertine, raking light slowly crosses the veining.
3. **Design:** plaster wall, a soft curved shadow slowly changes shape.
4. **Craft:** brushed oak planks, hand-held chisel/sander shavings drifting in the light (no hands visible).
5. **Reveal:** finished living room, a curtain lifts gently, one flower in a vase.

## 9. Materialidad (16:9) — fondo inmersivo
Macro of honed travertine surface filling the frame; a beam of raking light travels very slowly across it, revealing pores and veins. Optionally cut to oak grain and linen weave (3 shots of 5 s, cross-dissolve).

## 10. Consulta (4:5)
Warm bedroom at dusk; lamp glow, curtain moves with a soft breath of air. Static camera.

## 11. Transición opcional entre secciones (16:9, 3 s)
A door or arched opening slowly opens from a dark room into a bright one, the light spilling in. Use as intro overlay or between "Method" and "Services".
