The Alvera method

A slow, precise way of arriving.

01

Listen

We begin without a drawing. We spend time in your current spaces and learn how you wake, cook, host and rest — we understand how you live.

02

Concept

A narrative, a palette and a first library of materials. The idea of the home, written before it is drawn.

03

Design

Plans, elevations, renders and a considered furniture selection — every proportion tested against light.

04

Craft

Execution and site direction with the artisans and builders we trust. We stay on site until the details are right.

05

Reveal

Handover and final styling: books, ceramics, linen, one flower. We hand you a home that is ready to be lived in.
